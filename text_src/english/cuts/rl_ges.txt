1:After the Enclave fell, the money came back to the West Coast: gold from Redding, brahmin from the Republic, trade across the docks of San Francisco.
2:Where money flows, there are people who want to spend it. Your houses stood on every road it flowed along.
3:They were never clean. But whoever worked there got their share, a doctor, and a door that locked from the inside.
4:In the wasteland that was more than most had ever known. Word got around. The other houses had to follow suit or close.
5:When the Republic finally wrote laws for the trade, they read like your ledgers. Nobody said it out loud, and you never insisted.
6:You got rich. Whether you were a good person, the Den argued about for a long time. It was the first time anyone there had argued about such a thing.
